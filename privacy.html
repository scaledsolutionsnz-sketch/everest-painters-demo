Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Placeholder content — to be reviewed and confirmed before the site goes live. Last updated: August 2026.

1. Who we are

Everest Painters is a painting and decorating business based at Lake Hāwea, serving Wānaka and the wider Upper Clutha. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you ring or email — your name, phone number, email address and the address of the property.
  • Job information — what you would like painted, colour choices, access notes, and photographs of the work area.
  • Billing information needed to invoice you and record payment.
  • Basic website data — this site does not run advertising trackers. Standard hosting logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) may be recorded by our hosting provider for security and performance.

3. How we use it

  • To prepare quotes and carry out the work you have asked for.
  • To contact you about scheduling, access, colours or products.
  • To invoice you and keep required financial records.
  • To answer questions after the job, including any workmanship follow-up.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your say-so.

4. Third parties

We share information only where it is needed to do the job or run the business — for example a paint supplier tinting your colour, an accountant or bookkeeper, or a subcontractor engaged with your knowledge for a specific part of the work. Those parties are expected to keep your information confidential.

5. Embedded services on this site

This site embeds a Google Map so you can find us, and links out to Google, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. When you use those, the relevant provider's own privacy policy applies. There are no forms on this site — contact happens through the phone and email buttons, which open your own phone or email app.

6. Storage and security

Quotes, job notes and invoices are kept on password-protected devices and reputable cloud services. We keep information only as long as it is needed for the job and for the period required by tax and business record-keeping rules.

7. Photographs of work

We may photograph completed work for our portfolio or social media. We do not publish your name or address alongside those photos, and if you would rather we did not photograph your property at all, just tell us.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Get in touch and we will sort it out.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Call 0210 863 8636 or send us an email.
