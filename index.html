What we paint

Repaints, new work and the prep that makes it last.

Alpine sun, hard frosts and dry summers are rough on paint. Everything below starts with proper washing, sanding and sealing — that is the part that decides whether a finish lasts two years or ten.

01

Interior repaints

Walls, ceilings, doors, skirtings and window reveals. Furniture moved and covered, cut lines sharp, rooms handed back usable.

02

Exterior repaints

Weatherboard, plaster, fibre cement and joinery. Washed down, scraped, filled and sealed before topcoats go on.

03

Timber, cedar & decks

Oiling and staining for cedar cladding, schist-and-timber builds, pergolas, decks and fences that have gone grey in the sun.

04

Prep, patching & masking

Filling knocks and cracks, small gib patches, sanding back flaking paint, and masking so nothing lands where it shouldn't.

05

Rentals, holiday homes & commercial

Turnarounds between guests and tenants, shopfronts and offices — scheduled around bookings and worked to the day.

06

Colour matching & advice

Not sure between three whites? We bring the fan deck, talk through how each one behaves in Central Otago light, and test-patch it.

About Everest Painters

A local painting outfit that finishes what it starts.

Everest Painters is run by James out of Lake Hāwea, painting homes, cabins and small commercial jobs right across the Upper Clutha. You deal with James from the first look through to the last coat — the same person who quotes your job is on site while it is done.

Most of the work here is repaints: houses that have taken a decade of alpine sun on one side and frost on the other. Getting those right is about preparation, the correct product for the surface, and not rushing the last twenty percent.

  • Locally based — Lake Hāwea, working Wānaka to Luggate and Cardrona.
  • Clear written quotes — prep, products and coats spelled out before we start.
  • Tidy, respectful sites — covered floors, masked joinery, cleaned up daily.
