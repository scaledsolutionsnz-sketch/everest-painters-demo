The preparation behind a finish that lasts.

Central Otago's alpine sun, hard frosts and dry summers are tough on painted surfaces. Thorough washing, repairs, sanding, masking, priming and the correct coating system are what keep a finish looking sharp and performing properly for years.

Residential and commercial painting

Homes, rentals and holiday houses through to offices, clinics and shopfronts. Scheduled around your people and worked to the day.

Interior and exterior repaints

Walls, ceilings, joinery, weatherboard, plaster and fibre cement. Washed, scraped, filled and sealed before a topcoat goes near it.

New builds and full-property projects

Whole houses from bare substrate to final coat, staged around the other trades so the finish is not the thing holding up handover.

Roof spraying and specialist coatings

Sprayed roof coatings and the protective systems that suit alpine exposure, matched to the substrate rather than the price list.

Timber, cedar and deck finishes

Oiling and staining for cedar cladding, schist-and-timber builds, pergolas, decks and fences that have gone grey in the sun.

Decorative and textured finishes

Depth, texture and tonal work laid on by hand. Finishes that read as a surface in their own right, not just a colour on a wall.

Bespoke feature walls and murals

One-off walls designed around the room they live in, from a single considered colour block through to a full painted mural.

Commissioned artwork

Original pieces painted to brief for homes and commercial spaces. Bring an idea, a space and a rough size, and James will work it up.

Preparation, repairs and colour advice

Filling, patching, sanding and masking, plus a fan deck on site to test how a colour actually behaves in Central Otago light.
